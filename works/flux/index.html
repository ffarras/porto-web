About

Flux Energy empowers users to efficiently manage their energy consumption, offering insights on usage trends, token status, and providing a digital wallet for seamless transactions.

Flux Energy app was born from a simple idea: to help people understand and control their daily electricity use and monthly costs. It started as a personal mission and evolved into a tool for everyone to track their energy consumption and take charge of their electricity use.

Information Architecture

The app is organized into four main sections: Home, Usage, Wallet, and Profile.

Home
  • Usage Overview
  • Wallet Balance
  • Current bill or token information
  • Promo banners
Usage
  • Usage Statistic
  • Usage Trends and Estimation
  • Usage Comparison between Timeframes
Wallet
  • Wallet Balance
  • Top up and Withdraw Balance
  • Token and Bill Payment
  • Transaction History
Profile
  • App Profile Details
  • Edit App Profile
  • Transaction History
  • Payment Methods
  • Requests and Reports
  • Notification Settings
  • Help Center
  • Logout

Features

Stay in control of your energy consumption

Stay informed about your available tokens and daily energy consumption, receive timely reminders for your token and bill statuses, and keep an eye on your wallet balance.

Analyze your energy usage patterns

Dive into detailed data visualizations, track your consumption trends, and compare your energy consumption to improve efficiency.

Payments, all in your wallet

Buy tokens, pay bills, top up your balance, withdraw funds, and keep track of your transaction history in one convenient place.

Take control of your account

Manage electricity account, keep your payment methods up-to-date, and report maintenance issues during power interruptions.
